TELEGRAM (DrKLO/Telegram) - AVTOMATIK BUILD QO'LLANMASI
=========================================================

BU ZIP ENDI BARCHA 4 TA O'ZGARISHNI TO'LIQ AVTOMATIK BAJARADI:
1. Nomlanish tartibi (ism, keyin "bot"/"kanal"/"guruh")
2. Ulashish tugmasini TalkBack'dan yashirish
3. Fayl yuklash foizini e'lon qilish + yuklab olish tugaganda tovush
4. Kanal postlarida "Muhokamani ko'rish" menyu bandi

SIZDAN FAQAT IKKITA NARSA TALAB QILINADI:

1) api_id va api_hash (ALBATTA KERAK)
   - https://my.telegram.org saytiga kiring (o'z Telegram raqamingiz bilan)
   - "API development tools" bo'limidan yangi ilova yarating
   - api_config.txt faylini oching va shu formatda to'ldiring:

     API_ID=123456
     API_HASH=abcdef0123456789abcdef0123456789

2) google-services.json (IXTIYORIY - faqat push-bildirishnomalar uchun)
   - Bo'lmasa ham ilova ishlayveradi, faqat push-xabarnoma cheklangan bo'ladi
   - Kerak bo'lsa: Firebase konsolida (console.firebase.google.com) loyiha
     yaratib, google-services.json faylini yuklab, repo ildiziga qo'ying

REPO ILDIZIGA YUKLANADIGAN FAYLLAR (BARCHASI BU ZIP ICHIDA):
- api_config.txt (to'ldirib qo'ying)
- patch_dialogcell.py
- patch_sharebutton.py
- patch_progress.py
- patch_viewdiscussion.py
- .github/ papkasi (workflow bilan)
- google-services.json (ixtiyoriy, alohida qo'shasiz)

BUILD VARIANTI HAQIDA ESLATMA:
Workflow "assembleAfatRelease" buyrug'ini ishlatadi. Agar bu xato
bersa, Actions logida "assemble" so'zi bilan boshlanadigan variant
nomlarini toping va workflow faylidagi shu qatorni moslashtiring:
    run: ./gradlew assembleAfatRelease --stacktrace

IMZOLASH:
Birinchi build'da workflow o'zi imzolash kaliti (keystore) yaratib,
repo'ga saqlaydi - keyingi barcha buildlar shu bilan imzolanadi,
shuning uchun yangilanishlar to'g'ridan-to'g'ri o'rnatiladi
(eski ilovani o'chirish shart emas).

Settings -> Actions -> General -> "Workflow permissions" -> 
"Read and write permissions" yoqilgan bo'lishi shart (kalitni
va Release'ni saqlash uchun).
